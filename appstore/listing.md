## Description (4,000)

Tara School is a gentle learning game for young children. Tara means star in Hindi. Every instruction is spoken, so children who can't read yet can play on their own.

FOURTEEN GAMES
• Find the key: find a letter on the keyboard, in capitals or lowercase
• Follow the star: trace letters with a finger or Apple Pencil, following the stroke order children are taught (colouring in for Hindi and Arabic)
• Type a word: spell short words under a picture
• Starts with: hear a word and tap the letter it begins with, an early phonics skill
• Count: build numbers to 100 with tens and ones, or count and type how many
• Draw: draw freely with Apple Pencil, and keep every picture in a gallery
• Feed the animal: feed it exactly the number it asks for
• Add: count two groups together
• Find the pairs: a memory game with letters, numbers and pictures
• What's next?: finish the pattern
• Shapes and colours: listen, then tap the right one
• Sort: animals to one side, food to the other
• Say it: hear one word in every language your child is learning
• Piano: play freely, follow a song with a glowing key, or record a tune

FIVE LANGUAGES
English, French, Spanish, Hindi and Arabic, each spoken by a native voice. Choose the game's language, and the languages your child is learning.

TEN THEMES
Mermaids, unicorns, astronauts, dinosaurs, jungle, farm, rain, Diwali, Holi and Eid.

MADE FOR LITTLE ONES
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts
• Collects no data at all, and never connects to the internet
• Settings sit behind a question for grown-ups
• Mistakes get a gentle sound and a hint, never a penalty
• Stickers to collect along the way

## App Review notes

Tara School is a children's learning game that runs entirely offline from content bundled in the app. It has no accounts, network use, ads, purchases or third-party code.

To reach Settings, tap "For grown-ups" at the bottom of the home screen and answer the multiplication question shown.

The game is built with web technology inside a native app, so it runs fully offline with no web server. Drawings are saved as PNG files in the app's Documents folder, visible in the Files app.
