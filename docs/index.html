⭐ Tara School

Tara (तारा) means star in Hindi.

A gentle learning game for young children. Letters, writing, words, numbers to 100 and music, with every instruction spoken, so children who can't read yet can play on their own.

Play in your browser On the App Store soon

Five languages

English, French, Spanish, Hindi and Arabic, each spoken by a native voice. Choose the languages your child is learning.

Made for little ones

No ads, no purchases, no accounts. Mistakes get a gentle hint, never a penalty. Settings sit behind a question for grown-ups.

Private by design

The app collects nothing and never connects to the internet. Every word it says is recorded inside it.
