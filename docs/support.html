Tara School support

A spoken learning game for young children: letters, tracing, words, counting to 100, drawing, memory, patterns, shapes, sorting, languages and piano. It speaks English, French, Spanish, Hindi and Arabic, and works offline.

Help

  • Settings: tap For grown-ups at the bottom of the home screen and answer the sum. There you can set the child's name, the game's language, the languages they are learning, and the theme.
  • How to play: each game explains itself out loud the first time, and again whenever the child taps ❓. Settings also lists how every game works.
  • Saved drawings: open the Files app, then On My iPad, then Tara School, then Drawings.
  • No sound? Check the volume buttons on the side of the iPad.
