Tara School privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Tara School is a learning game for young children. It collects no personal information, and it sends nothing anywhere.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It never connects to the internet: every word it speaks is recorded and stored inside the app.

What stays on the device

A few things are saved on the iPad so the game remembers them between visits. They never leave the device:

  • a first name, if a grown-up types one in Settings, used so the child can practise typing and tracing it
  • the chosen language, theme and level, and the stickers the child has earned
  • drawings the child saves, kept as picture files in the app's own folder, which a grown-up can see in the Files app

Deleting the app deletes all of these.

Children

The app is made for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to share, sell or disclose. The settings screen is behind a question for grown-ups.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions are welcome through the project's GitHub page.
